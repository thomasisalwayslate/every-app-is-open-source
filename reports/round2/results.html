Limits

Three trials per arm on one ARM64 game estimate variability only. Full correctness leaves an accuracy ceiling: this study cannot establish better reasoning accuracy. The new build changes damage/armor ordering thresholds, ammo fallback comparisons, pickup scaling, splash attenuation and BFG loop bounds. Executable probes independently agree with all 24 frozen answer fields. This is static analysis, without a WAD/gameplay session.

No tools, questions or grader changes occurred after the protocol was frozen. Earlier results remain unchanged. No general superiority claim follows from this study.
